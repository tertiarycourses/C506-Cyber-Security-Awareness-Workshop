# Lab-01: Threat Triage with the Cybersecurity Simulator

**Course:** Cyber Security Awareness Workshop  
**Course code:** C506  
**Time:** 25 minutes  
**Alignment:** LO1  
**Tool:** [Cybersecurity Simulator](https://alfredang.github.io/cybersecuritysimulator/)

## Scenario

A small Singapore design agency receives five mixed security alerts. Decide what is benign, suspicious or urgent.

## Safety and authorisation boundary

- Use only the named training tool and the mock files in this folder.
- Do not enter real passwords, personal data, payment details or confidential information.
- Do not test any other website, network, account or organisation.
- Stop immediately if the target, data or behaviour differs from the written scope.
- Prefer reversible actions and preserve synthetic evidence.

## Mock data

- `mock-threat-events.csv`

## Detailed procedure

1. Open mock-threat-events.csv and read all five events before classifying any one event.
2. Open the Cybersecurity Simulator and select the closest scenario for each event.
3. For every event, record the asset, threat actor or mechanism, observable signal and potential business impact.
4. Assign a severity from 1 to 5 using likelihood and impact; explain one uncertainty in your score.
5. Choose the first safe action: observe, contain, verify with the user, isolate the device, or escalate.
6. Capture one screenshot of your highest-risk decision and export your completed triage table.

## Completion standard

- Completed triage table
- Severity rationale
- Screenshot of highest-risk decision
- One escalation statement

## Reflection

What evidence would make you reverse your decision, and who would need to know?
