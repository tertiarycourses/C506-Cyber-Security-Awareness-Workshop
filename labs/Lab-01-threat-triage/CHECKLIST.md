# Lab-01 Checklist

## Before

- [ ] I am using only supplied mock data.
- [ ] I confirmed the tool URL and training scope.
- [ ] I will stop if the environment differs from the instructions.

## During

- [ ] Step 1: Open mock-threat-events.csv and read all five events before classifying any one event.
- [ ] Step 2: Open the Cybersecurity Simulator and select the closest scenario for each event.
- [ ] Step 3: For every event, record the asset, threat actor or mechanism, observable signal and potential business impact.
- [ ] Step 4: Assign a severity from 1 to 5 using likelihood and impact; explain one uncertainty in your score.
- [ ] Step 5: Choose the first safe action: observe, contain, verify with the user, isolate the device, or escalate.
- [ ] Step 6: Capture one screenshot of your highest-risk decision and export your completed triage table.

## Before submission

- [ ] Completed triage table
- [ ] Severity rationale
- [ ] Screenshot of highest-risk decision
- [ ] One escalation statement
- [ ] Screenshots are redacted and contain no real personal or secret data.
- [ ] My conclusion distinguishes fact from inference.
