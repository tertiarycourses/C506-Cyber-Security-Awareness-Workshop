# Lab-10: Integrated Incident Response and Escalation

**Course:** Cyber Security Awareness Workshop  
**Course code:** C506  
**Time:** 45 minutes  
**Alignment:** LO1–LO3  
**Tool:** [Integrated toolkit](https://alfredang.github.io/cybersecuritysimulator/)

## Scenario

A supplier-payment email, endpoint alert and suspicious network connection may form one business-impacting incident.

## Safety and authorisation boundary

- Use only the named training tool and the mock files in this folder.
- Do not enter real passwords, personal data, payment details or confidential information.
- Do not test any other website, network, account or organisation.
- Stop immediately if the target, data or behaviour differs from the written scope.
- Prefer reversible actions and preserve synthetic evidence.

## Mock data

- `mock-incident-brief.md`
- `mock-timeline.csv`
- `mock-escalation-contacts.csv`
- `mock-risk-register.csv`

## Detailed procedure

1. Read the incident brief and assign roles: incident lead, evidence recorder, communications lead and business owner.
2. Correlate email, endpoint, identity and network events into one timeline; state which events are facts and which are hypotheses.
3. Assess impact to confidentiality, integrity, availability, safety, finance, reputation and legal obligations.
4. Choose immediate actions that are reversible, evidence-preserving and proportionate to business risk.
5. Use the contact matrix to escalate to the correct internal and external parties; do not contact real organisations during the exercise.
6. Deliver a three-minute incident briefing: situation, evidence, impact, actions, decisions required and next update time.
7. Complete the mock risk register and one after-action improvement with owner and due date.

## Completion standard

- Correlated timeline
- CIA and business-impact assessment
- Decision log
- Escalation record
- Three-minute briefing
- After-action improvement

## Reflection

What evidence would make you reverse your decision, and who would need to know?
